# Support

## Contact

The official and preferred support channel is:

[gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com)

Messages are usually answered within 1–2 business days, in English or Bulgarian.

## What to Include

For a technical problem, include only what is needed:

- iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version
- App version (shown in the app’s settings)
- The mode you were playing — Plant a Garden, Plant Sudoku, or Garden Puzzles — and a short description of what happened
- A screenshot, only if it does not show anything you would rather not share

Support correspondence and any necessary notes are normally kept for six months after the case is resolved — see the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/ten-turn-garden/en/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).

## Full Garden

Full Garden is a one-time purchase, and there is no subscription to cancel.

- **Full Garden missing after reinstalling or changing device:** open the app’s settings, go to Full Garden, and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
- **Family Sharing:** Full Garden can be shared with your family group. A family member may need to tap “Restore purchases” once on their own device.
- **Refunds:** Apple decides refunds under its own policies. Use Apple’s “Report a Problem” page (reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Charged but nothing unlocked:** try “Restore purchases” first, then write to us with the date of the purchase — never with card or bank details.

Please never include payment card or bank information in a support message.

## Progress, Streaks, and iCloud

Your medals, journal, discoveries, and streak are kept in your iCloud unless you turned that off in Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ “Keep my garden in iCloud”. On a new or reinstalled device signed in to the same Apple Account, they come back on their own.

- **Progress not showing on another device:** check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that the switch is on in both. Then close and reopen the game; iCloud can take a minute.
- **The switch turned itself off:** this happens when the iCloud account on the device changes, so one account’s progress never reaches another’s. Turn it on again if the account is yours.
- **Settings and a Plant Sudoku game in play** are not copied to iCloud; they stay on each device.

We hold no copy of your progress and cannot see your iCloud, so support cannot recover progress that was never in iCloud — for example with the switch turned off — or that iCloud did not keep. Full Garden is restored separately, with “Restore purchases”.

Today’s garden changes at midnight UTC. If your streak looks wrong, check that your device’s date and time are set automatically.
