# About Ten-Turn Garden

## A Small, Quiet Puzzle Game

Ten-Turn Garden is a calm strategy game made to be played slowly. You plant ten plants on a small garden board; neighbours that grow well together score as pairs, and the right three together make a named combination. A game takes a minute or two, and every garden is different.

There are three ways to play:

- **Plant a Garden** — free play, challenges, and today’s garden, the same for everyone each day, with medals, a streak, and a garden journal
- **Plant Sudoku** — a botanical twist on sudoku
- **Garden Puzzles** — hand-made puzzles arranged in chapters

Ten-Turn Garden is available for iPhone and iPad, in English and Bulgarian.

## Quiet by Design

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no energy or timers, and no server. Your garden stays on your device, and your medals, journal, and streak can follow you to your other devices through your own iCloud — never through us. The [Privacy Policy]({{ '/ten-turn-garden/en/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}) says exactly what that means.

## Full Garden

The game is free to play. **Full Garden** is a single optional **one-time purchase** that unlocks more plants, unlimited challenges and daily gardens, more Plant Sudoku levels, extra garden skins, and the rest of the Garden Puzzles. There is no subscription, and it can be shared through Apple Family Sharing.
