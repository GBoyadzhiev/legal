# Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 29, 2026

## 1. Who Is Responsible

Ten-Turn Garden is published by **Georgi Boyadzhiev**, acting as an individual (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). For the limited personal data we actually receive, the Developer is the data controller.

Contact us at [gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com). Email is the official and preferred contact channel.

## 2. Scope

This Policy covers the Ten-Turn Garden app for iPhone and iPad, its in-app purchase, support communications, and these legal pages.

## 3. The Short Version

Ten-Turn Garden does not require an account. It never asks for a name, an email address, a date of birth, or any other personal detail, and we do not collect or receive personal data about the person playing it.

The app has **no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no third-party code, and no tracking**. It does not connect to any server of ours — there is no server. The only thing that can leave the device is your game progress, copied to your own iCloud account through Apple (section 5), which we cannot see. We do not build profiles, and we do not sell or share personal data.

## 4. What the App Keeps on the Device

The app keeps the following in its own storage on the device:

- Game progress — Garden Puzzles progress, a Plant Sudoku game in play, and whether the tutorial has been completed
- Today’s gardens — best scores, medals, the streak, and the garden journal
- Discoveries — plant combinations you have found
- Settings — sound, haptics, motion, hints, difficulty level, language, and garden skin

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see, recover, or delete it. Removing the app removes it from the device, subject to any device backup you have set up with Apple under Apple’s terms. Part of it can also be kept in your iCloud, as described next.

## 5. Your Progress in iCloud

So that you can carry on from another device or after reinstalling, the app keeps a copy of your earned progress in **your own iCloud account**, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. The copy holds:

- best scores and medals from today’s gardens, and the streak
- Garden Puzzles medals and the puzzle types you have played
- the garden journal and the plant combinations you have discovered
- whether the tutorials have been completed

Settings, a Plant Sudoku game in play, and your recent plant choices stay on the device and are not copied. The copy contains no name, email address, or other personal detail, and the app is given no Apple Account details.

This is **on unless you turn it off**: Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ “Keep my garden in iCloud”. Turning it off stops copying; it does not delete what is already in iCloud. If the iCloud account on the device changes, the app turns the switch off so that one account’s progress is never copied into another’s. Without an iCloud account, progress simply stays on the device.

The copy is stored and transmitted by Apple under Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy. It goes from your device to your iCloud account directly — it never passes through us, and **we cannot see, access, recover, or delete it**. The app does not currently offer a way to erase the iCloud copy itself; turning the switch off on every device stops it from being used or updated.

## 6. What the App Does Not Do

To be explicit, Ten-Turn Garden does not:

- ask for or accept a name, email address, age, photo, or any other personal detail
- include accounts, chat, leaderboards, Game Center, or any other online or social feature
- copy anything other than the game progress described in section 5 to iCloud, or use any other cloud service
- use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, calendar, or location
- send notifications
- show advertising of any kind

## 7. Ratings and Reviews

At a good moment the app may ask whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is shown by Apple’s operating system, which decides how often it appears; we do not learn whether it was shown or what you chose. A rating or review you submit is handled by Apple under its own terms.

## 8. In-App Purchase

“Full Garden” is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment, the payment method, taxes, and any refund. **We never receive card, bank, or Apple Account details.**

To know whether Full Garden is unlocked, the app asks the App Store on the device whether the current Apple Account owns it. Apple may give us aggregate sales figures and the information needed to resolve a purchase problem. Apple’s handling of purchases is described in Apple’s own privacy information.

## 9. Children

Ten-Turn Garden is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, there is no personal data of a child for us to receive through the app.

If you believe a child has sent us personal data — for example by emailing support — contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Information We Receive Directly

The only way we receive personal data is if you write to us.

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to attach, such as a screenshot or device and app details. We use it to answer you, to fix problems, and to meet legal obligations. Depending on the request, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in supporting and improving the app, steps related to our agreement with you, compliance with law, or your consent where required.

Please include no more personal information than the question needs. Support correspondence and any necessary notes are normally deleted within **six months after the case is resolved**, unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary for an active dispute or legal claim.

## 11. These Legal Pages

These pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, and processes that data under its own privacy terms.

Support email is provided through Google, which may process email metadata and content to deliver that service under the Google Privacy Policy.

## 12. Recipients and International Transfers

We do not sell personal data. Personal data you send to support may be processed by Google as our email provider and, where necessary, by professional advisers or public authorities. Apple processes App Store activity and stores the iCloud copy of your progress in your own account, and GitHub hosts these legal pages.

Apple, Google, and GitHub may process data outside your country. Their own privacy notices describe their locations and transfer safeguards. We disclose support data to anyone else only where necessary for the purposes above or required by law.

## 13. Security

We apply reasonable organizational and technical measures to the data we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Because your progress lives on your device and, if the switch is on, in your iCloud account, protecting the device and your Apple Account — a passcode, current software, and two-factor authentication — is the most effective safeguard.

## 14. Your Rights

Data kept by the app is controlled directly by you: settings can be changed in the app, the iCloud copy can be switched off in the app’s settings, and removing the app removes its local storage.

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies to personal data we hold — in practice, support correspondence — you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Some rights depend on the circumstances and on legal exceptions. Write to the email address above; we may need reasonable information to locate your request.

Because we never receive the app’s data — neither what is on the device nor the copy in your iCloud — a support request cannot retrieve, change, or erase your game progress.

You may lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or with the supervisory authority where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred.

## 15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. For material changes, we will give reasonable notice through an app update or another appropriate channel before or when the change takes effect, and will request consent where the law requires it. Minor corrections may be reflected by updating the date above.

## 16. Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests should be sent to [gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com).
