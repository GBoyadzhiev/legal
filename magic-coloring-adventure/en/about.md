# About Magic Coloring Adventure

## A Cozy Place to Color

Magic Coloring Adventure is a coloring app made for children, with books full of cute animals, fairy tales, dinosaurs, vehicles, and other friendly worlds. Every drawing uses bold outlines and simple shapes designed for little hands: tap to fill, zoom in, and undo without any way to get stuck.

It is available for iPhone and iPad, and for Android devices.

## Quiet by Design

There are no ads, no advertising network, no analytics, and no tracking. The app asks for no account, no name, and no email address, and it has no chat, comments, or other social features.

Coloring progress and settings stay on the device. A finished picture can be saved to the photo library when someone chooses to save it, and nothing else leaves the app. Purchases and settings sit behind a press-and-hold parental gate meant for an adult.

## Books and Purchases

The app comes with pages to color, and additional themed books are available as **one-time purchases** through the App Store or Google Play. There are no subscriptions and nothing renews. Purchases can be restored on a new device with “Restore Purchases”.

## A Note on the Artwork

Some of the artwork was created with the help of AI tools, and every image is reviewed and refined before it reaches the app, to make sure it is appropriate, safe, and enjoyable for children. The full note is in the [Disclaimer]({{ '/magic-coloring-adventure/en/disclaimer/' | relative_url }}).
