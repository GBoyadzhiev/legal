# Support

## Contact

The official and preferred support channel is:

[gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com)

## What to Include

For a technical issue, include only what is needed:

- Device model and iOS version
- Litsi version
- A concise description and reproduction steps
- A screenshot only if it does not expose information you do not want to share

Do **not** send journal entries, exported archives, health information, or other sensitive content unless it is strictly necessary and you choose to do so. Support cannot remotely access, inspect, recover, or delete journal data stored only on your device.

Support correspondence and necessary case notes are normally retained for six months after resolution. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/litsi/en/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}) for details.

## Local Data and Backups

Journal data is stored locally. Before deleting Litsi, erasing or replacing a device, use the app’s backup export and keep the resulting unencrypted archive in a secure location. Support cannot recover missing journal data. If you have a usable archive, you may import it through Litsi.

## Purchases

- Manage or cancel: [Apple subscription management](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)
- Request a refund: [Apple Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)
- Restore an eligible entitlement: use “Restore Purchases” in Litsi

Apple handles payments and refund decisions. Include no payment-card or bank details in a support message.

## Safety

Litsi support does not provide medical, psychological, therapeutic, or crisis assistance. Litsi is a self-reflection tool only. For urgent danger or a health crisis, contact local emergency services or an appropriate qualified professional.
