# Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 20, 2026

## 1. Who Is Responsible

Litsi is operated by **Georgi Boyadzhiev**, acting as an individual (the “Operator”, “we”, “us”). For personal data that we actually receive, the Operator is the data controller.

Contact the Operator at [gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com). The legally required provider identity, address, and correspondence details are centralized in the [Terms of Use]({{ '/litsi/en/terms-of-use/' | relative_url }}).

## 2. Scope and Privacy Design

This Policy covers the Litsi iOS application, its App Store subscriptions, support communications, and Litsi legal pages.

Litsi does not require an account. Journal entries, moods, activities, reflections, and related journal data are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to a server operated by us, and we cannot remotely view, recover, export, correct, or delete them.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. We do not create advertising profiles or sell personal data.

## 3. Data Processed in the App

### Local journal data

Journal data is processed on your device to provide the Journal, Calendar, Insights, backup, sharing, and eligible AI Beta Recap features. We do not receive this data.

Your device and operating system may include app data in an iCloud or device backup according to your Apple settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

### AI Beta Recap

On supported devices, AI Beta Recap uses Apple’s on-device models and locally derived journal observations. Journal data is not sent to us or to an external AI provider for this feature, and we do not use it to train an external AI system.

AI Beta Recap may be unavailable depending on the device, operating system, language, region, Apple Intelligence settings, or model readiness. Generated recap text is currently available only in English.

### Backup, import, and export

Backup and restore are user-initiated. An exported Litsi archive is an **unencrypted, readable file**. You choose where it is saved or shared. Once it leaves Litsi, the selected destination’s privacy and security practices apply.

You are responsible for keeping suitable backup copies and protecting exported files. Deleting Litsi, erasing or replacing a device, losing a device, or deleting local data may permanently remove journal data if no usable backup exists.

### Shareable content

Litsi creates a share image only when you choose to do so. You decide whether and where to share it. The receiving app, service, and audience process that content under their own terms and privacy practices. Review the image before sharing it.

### Reminders

If you enable reminders, Litsi schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder text may be visible on the lock screen according to your notification settings. We do not receive your reminder schedule.

## 4. Data We May Receive

### Support communications

If you email or call support, we may receive your name, contact details, message, device and app details, and any files or screenshots you choose to provide. Calls are not recorded. We may keep limited case notes needed to resolve the request.

Please do not send journal entries, exported archives, health information, or other sensitive content unless it is strictly necessary for the support issue and you choose to do so. We cannot inspect journal data that remains only inside the app.

We use support data to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, protect legal rights, and meet legal obligations. Depending on the request, the legal basis is performance of or steps related to our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in supporting and securing Litsi, compliance with law, or your consent where required.

Support correspondence and necessary case notes are normally deleted within **six months after the case is resolved**, unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary for an active dispute or legal claim.

### App Store purchases

Apple processes purchases, payment details, tax, refunds, and subscription administration. Litsi reads StoreKit product and entitlement status on your device to unlock features. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank details. Apple may provide us with aggregate sales information and information needed to address a purchase issue.

See [Apple’s App Store privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/) and [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Optional Apple diagnostics

If you choose in your Apple device settings to share analytics or diagnostics with developers, Apple may provide diagnostic information governed by Apple’s settings and policies. Litsi does not include its own third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

### Legal pages

These pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy terms. See [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Email provider

Support email is provided through Google. Google may process email metadata and content to deliver that service under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Recipients and International Transfers

We do not sell personal data. Personal data that you send to support may be processed by Google as our email provider and, where necessary, by professional advisers or public authorities. Apple processes App Store activity, and GitHub hosts these legal pages.

Apple, Google, and GitHub may process data outside your country. Their own privacy notices describe their locations and transfer safeguards. We disclose support data to another recipient only when necessary for the purposes above or required by law.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical measures for data we receive. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with an appropriate passcode, keep iOS updated, restrict lock-screen notification previews if needed, and store exported archives in a secure location.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage local journal data directly in the app, export a backup, delete entries, disable reminders, control notification previews, and choose not to share content. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to any separate device or cloud backup managed through Apple.

Where the GDPR or similar law applies to personal data we hold, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some rights depend on the circumstances and legal exceptions. Contact us using the primary email above. We may need reasonable information to verify and locate your request.

Because we do not hold your local journal database, a support request cannot retrieve, modify, or erase journal data that remains only on your device.

You may lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian [Commission for Personal Data Protection](https://cpdp.bg/en/lodging-complaints-and-alerts/) or with the supervisory authority in your country of habitual residence, work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

## 8. Age Requirement

Litsi is intended only for people aged **18 or older**. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has contacted support, please notify us.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice through an app update, a one-time in-app notice, or another appropriate channel before or when the change takes effect. We will request consent where the law requires it. Minor corrections may be reflected by updating the date above.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests should be sent to [gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com](mailto:gbindie.dev+support@gmail.com). Email is the official and preferred contact channel.
